# Firefly III: multi-currency transactions show the wrong converted amount — fixed in 6.7.0

## Symptom

When "Convert amounts to your primary currency" is enabled, the `native_amount` shown for a transaction does not match the exchange rate that was in effect on the transaction's date. Running `correction:recalculate-pc-amounts` resets every transaction's converted amount to use today's rate instead of each transaction's own date, so historical transactions all end up converted at the same, current rate.

## Affects

Users with "Convert amounts to your primary currency" enabled who hold transactions in a foreign currency dated in the past, especially after running the `correction:recalculate-pc-amounts` command. Not an issue for users who only transact in their primary currency.

## Cause

`ConvertsAmountToPrimaryAmount::convert()` calls the exchange rate converter with `now()` instead of the transaction journal's date, even though the date is already available and the converter supports historical lookups.

## Remedy

Update to Firefly III 6.7.0 or later, which uses the transaction's own date when converting to the primary currency. After updating, re-run the recalculation so previously affected transactions get corrected.

## Notes

Distinct from the Subscriptions widget multi-currency total issue and the weekly-subscription-skip issue — this one affects the native_amount conversion on ordinary transactions.
