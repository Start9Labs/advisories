# ERPNext: Price List doesn't update or clear when switching Customer/Supplier on an order — fixed in 15.121.3

## Symptom

On a Sales Order or Purchase Order, changing the Customer or Supplier leaves the previous party's Price List selected when the newly selected party has no default Price List — instead of switching to the configured default or clearing the field.

## Affects

Anyone creating or editing a Sales Order or Purchase Order who switches the Customer or Supplier to one without its own default Price List (and, for customers, no Customer Group default either). Not limited to any particular Price List configuration.

## Cause

The form did not re-evaluate the Price List fallback logic when the party changed, so it kept whatever Price List was set for the previously selected party.

## Remedy

Update to ERPNext 15.121.3 or later, which applies the correct default Price List (or clears the field when no default applies) whenever the Customer or Supplier is changed.

## Notes

Existing party-specific, Customer Group, POS, and permission-based Price List precedence is preserved by the fix.
