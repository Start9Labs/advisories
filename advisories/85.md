# Firefly III 6.7.2: Check for updates page fails with "Call to undefined function _()" — fix coming in 6.7.3

## Symptom

Navigating to Settings and clicking "Check for updates" fails with an error page. The underlying error is `Call to undefined function _() (View: /var/www/html/resources/views/settings/update/index.blade.php)`.

## Affects

All users on Firefly III 6.7.2 who use the "Check for updates" button in Settings.

## Cause

An incorrect translation-helper prefix in the update-check view.

## Remedy

This is fixed and will ship in Firefly III 6.7.3. Until then, avoid the "Check for updates" page — it does not affect any other functionality, and update checks can be skipped safely.

## Notes

Confirmed by the maintainer (JC5), who merged the fix and tied it to the next release, milestoned for 6.7.3. StartOS currently ships 6.7.2, 6.7.1 and 6.6.6; this affects anyone on 6.7.2.
