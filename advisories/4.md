# Backups of database-backed services fail with `cp terminated with signal SIGKILL` — a 30-second cap in the SDK, not the OOM killer

## Symptom

`Failed: Unknown Error: Error: cp terminated with signal SIGKILL:` — with nothing after the colon. The backup dies after one to five minutes, far too fast to have copied the service's data. Other services in the same run succeed. It fails identically on every target: internal drive, USB disk, Network Folder. Often intermittent before becoming permanent.

## Affects

StartOS 0.4.0 and 0.4.0.1, backing up a service with a database — btcpayserver, cal-diy, ghost, immich, mattermost, nextcloud, spliit, synapse, and the community packages bunker46, buzz-relay, dojo, lawallet and readstr — to any target.

## Cause

A service with a database backs it up by dumping it to a file, then copying that file to the backup target. That copy has a fixed 30-second limit in our SDK, so once the database is large enough for the copy to exceed it, the copy is killed. Total run time is the dump's duration plus exactly 30 seconds, which is why the total varies while the failure does not.

## Remedy

There is nothing reliable on the current release — be straight about that. The fix ships in StartOS **0.4.0.2** together with an updated version of the affected service, and both are needed, since the timer lives in the service package.

**Tell them to stop retrying, and that their last good backup may already be damaged.** The dump is written directly over the previous one, so a failed attempt leaves it half-written while StartOS still shows the backup as present, with the right date. Retrying cannot produce a good backup and may destroy one that is still intact. Files already on the target are untouched — the failure happens before any file copying begins. Restoring runs the same copy under the same limit, so do not promise a restore will work either. This is the most important thing in this entry.

## Do not

**This is not the OOM killer and not a memory problem**, whatever the server's RAM. The kill comes from our own timer in ordinary software, so there is no kernel OOM event. Do not ask about RAM, `free -m`, other running services, or `dmesg`. Do not suggest a different drive, enclosure, cable, or backup target — the limit is target-independent, and one customer nearly bought a replacement SSD over this. Do not suggest disabling service features such as Immich's smart search; the service is stopped for the duration of the backup.

## Notes

- StartOS 0.4.0 and 0.4.0.1. Affected services: **btcpayserver, cal-diy, ghost, immich, mattermost, nextcloud, spliit, synapse**, and community bunker46, buzz-relay, dojo, lawallet, readstr.
- **"My other services back up fine" is expected and is not a clue.** Services without a database never make this copy, so it does not narrow the problem to the failing service.
- Immich, Nextcloud and Synapse report it most, their databases being the largest. Immich's is larger than its photo count suggests, as its machine-learning search data lives there too.

Known since 2026-08-25.
