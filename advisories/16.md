# Manual StartTunnel port forwards can be deleted after updating

## Symptom

Duplicate forwards — the old manual ones alongside the new automatic ones.

## Affects

Anyone who created manual forwards in StartTunnel and has since updated both. In practice that means updating StartOS to 0.4.0.1, since beta.10 itself is not offered as an update target.

## Cause

StartOS creates port forwards automatically as of 0.4.0-beta.10 with StartTunnel 1.2.0. Before that they were manual.

## Remedy

Delete the manual forwards. To trigger the automatic ones, toggle the interface address off and on, restart the server, or wait up to 5 minutes.

## Notes

Known since 2026-07-23.
