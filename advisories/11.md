# Cannot log in over Tor on 0.4.0

## Symptom

Login fails with `RPC ERROR: Unauthorized. Invalid request signature: not valid for any identity this server recognizes (hostname, domain, or IP address); the request was likely signed for a different server identity or by an incompatible client.`

## Affects

StartOS 0.4.0 reached over Tor at its https:// address. Fixed in 0.4.0.1.

## Cause

A bug in the signature auth scheme introduced in 0.4.0 makes it impossible to log in over Tor.

## Remedy

Log in over LAN, .local, or a VPN gateway instead of Tor, then update to 0.4.0.1. Tor login works again once the update completes.

## Notes

Known since 2026-07-25.
