# Jellyfin 12.0: Parental Control blocks entire library, not just restricted content — fixed in 12.1

## Symptom

A user with a "Maximum allowed parental rating" set cannot play any content in a library, even content rated at or below their allowed limit — as long as that library contains at least one item rated above the limit. The affected item is unplayable regardless of its own rating once the whole library is blocked this way.

## Affects

Jellyfin 12.0, servers using per-item Parental Ratings together with a user's "Maximum allowed parental rating" setting, on libraries that mix content above and below that rating. Did not occur on the 12.0 release candidates (RC1–RC4); it appeared by the 12.0 final release.

## Cause

Parental rating filtering was applied at the library level instead of per item, so one over-limit item in a library blocked the whole library for that user.

## Remedy

Update to Jellyfin 12.1, which fixes parental rating filtering to apply per item rather than per library.

## Do not

Do not tell the customer to remove ratings from the offending items or to split libraries — that changes their catalog organization and isn't the actual fix.

## Notes

Reported against a fresh 12.0 install upgraded from RC4; behavior on earlier RCs was correct.
