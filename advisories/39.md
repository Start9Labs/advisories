# Firefly III: editing a budget's amount deletes its notes

## Symptom

Notes added to a budget disappear after the budgeted amount for that period is changed.

## Affects

Users of Firefly III v6.6.6 and earlier who add notes to a budget and later edit the budgeted amount.

## Cause

Confirmed by a maintainer; the amount-update logic clears the note.

## Remedy

Fixed for release in v6.7.0. Until then, re-add the note after changing a budget's amount, or change the amount first and add the note afterward.

## Notes

Milestone firefly-iii-v6.7.0; no release date given.
