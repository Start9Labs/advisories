# ERPNext: partial return on a Purchase Invoice without Update Stock fails with "StockOverReturnError" — fixed in 15.121.3

## Symptom

Returning less than the full billed quantity against a Purchase Invoice that does not have Update Stock enabled fails, and the rest of the invoice's quantity can no longer be returned either — blocked with a stock over-return error.

## Affects

Purchase Invoices created without Update Stock (the ordinary, non-stock invoice path). Returns against Purchase Receipts, Subcontracting Receipts, or a Purchase Invoice with Update Stock enabled are not affected.

## Cause

Received/rejected quantity is only meant to be tracked where an accepted/rejected split exists — Purchase Receipts, Subcontracting Receipts, and stock-updating Purchase Invoices. For a non-stock Purchase Invoice, that validation never ran, so whatever value was last left in the read-only received quantity field was wrongly counted as already returned, understating how much of the invoice was still returnable.

## Remedy

Update to ERPNext 15.121.3 or later, which counts received/rejected quantity only where that split genuinely applies; a non-stock Purchase Invoice's returns are validated against billed quantity alone.

## Notes

The desk form on a non-stock Purchase Invoice also stops showing the Received Qty/Rejected Qty/warehouse fields on returns, matching how an ordinary (non-return) non-stock invoice already behaves.
