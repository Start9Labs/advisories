# Firefly III: Fiscal Year setting shows disabled with no date after upgrading — fixed in 6.7.2

## Symptom

After upgrading Firefly III, the Fiscal Year setting under Preferences > General shows as disabled with no start date, even though it was previously enabled. Re-enabling it and saving does not make it display as enabled either. The underlying preference is still correctly stored in the database — this is a display issue, not data loss.

## Affects

Anyone who had the Fiscal Year preference enabled before upgrading to 6.7.1. The setting's stored value is unaffected; only its display on the Preferences page is wrong.

## Remedy

Update to Firefly III 6.7.2 or later, once available. No data repair is needed — the fiscal year preference itself was never lost.

## Do not

- Do not re-save or reconfigure the fiscal year dates as a fix — the stored preference is already correct; only the settings page display is affected.

## Notes

Confirmed by a maintainer and labeled fixed, targeted for milestone v6.7.2. No release date has been given.
