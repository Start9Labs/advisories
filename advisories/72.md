# Electrs freezes for all wallets when one Electrum client stops reading — do not reinstall, update to 0.11.1:20

## Symptom

Electrs stops indexing new blocks and stops serving every connected wallet at once — Sparrow reports "Retries exhausted", Electrum, Trezor Suite, BlueWallet and others time out with no TLS or certificate error. Electrs itself still shows "Electrum Server is ready and accepting connections" and appears fully synced. The stall lasts anywhere from under an hour to over 8 hours, then recovers on its own with a burst of `disconnecting due to failed to send response` log lines followed by a catch-up batch like `indexing 58 blocks`. Stopping the service during a stall can require a forced kill after it fails to respond to a normal stop.

## Affects

Any server running Electrus versions carrying the build tagged `:17` or older (including `0.11.1:17` and earlier revisions of `0.11.1`) — anyone with an Electrum client that stops draining its socket (a suspended app, a device that dropped off network without closing the connection, a stalled proxy) can trigger it for every other wallet connected to the same server. Not present on `0.11.1:18` and later.

## Cause

Electrs wrote responses to client sockets with a blocking write and no timeout, on the same single-threaded loop that also does block indexing. One client that stopped reading — without closing the connection — held that loop hostage until the kernel eventually gave up on the socket, freezing indexing and every other wallet's connection along with it.

## Remedy

Update to Electrs 0.11.1:20, which carries a fix (present since 0.11.1:18) bounding each client write to a 60-second timeout, so a stalled client is disconnected on its own instead of freezing the server. No reindex or other cleanup is needed after updating — the fix does not touch the index.

## Do not

- Do not reinstall or wipe the index to fix a stall like this — the freeze is not a corrupted or stuck resync; a reinstall is a many-hour rebuild that was never necessary.
- Do not read "Fully synced" followed shortly by a stall as the sync having failed — it had genuinely finished; a single wedged peer froze it afterward.

## Notes

The giveaway that this is the wedged-peer freeze rather than a real sync problem: recovery ends in a burst of `disconnecting due to failed to send response` lines immediately followed by an indexing catch-up batch. Short dips under roughly 40 minutes with no such burst are unrelated ordinary indexing blips.
