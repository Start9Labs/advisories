# StartOS and StartTunnel updates log you out

## Symptom

You are logged out immediately after the update.

## Affects

Updating StartOS from 0.4.0-beta.9 or earlier to 0.4.0-beta.10 or newer (in practice: to 0.4.0 or 0.4.0.1), or StartTunnel from 1.1.3 or earlier to 1.2.0 or newer.

## Cause

Cookie auth was replaced with signature auth. Existing sessions don't survive it.

## Remedy

Log back in. Nothing is wrong.

## Notes

Known since 2026-07-23.
