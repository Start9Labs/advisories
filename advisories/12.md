# Service missing after updating from 0.3.5.1 to 0.4.0

## Symptom

A previously installed service is gone after the update, and a "Service Failed to Migrate" notification with the error is in the Notifications tab.

## Affects

Servers updated from 0.3.5.1 to 0.4.0.

## Cause

The service failed to migrate during the update.

## Remedy

Reinstall the service. Its data is preserved from the original install.

## Notes

Known since 2026-07-25.
