# Sparrow, Webtop, and Bitcoin testnet services moved to a third-party registry

## Symptom

The service is missing from the Start9 marketplace.

## Affects

Sparrow, Webtop, and all Bitcoin testnet services.

## Cause

These services are no longer published by Start9.

## Remedy

Add the third-party registry at https://registry.testnet4.info. These services are not maintained by Start9 and we do not vouch for them.

## Notes

Known since 2026-07-26.
