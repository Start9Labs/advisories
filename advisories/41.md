# Firefly III: weekly subscriptions with multiple skips understate the amount still due that month

## Symptom

After paying a weekly subscription that skips 2 or more weeks, the subscription is shown as fully paid for the month even though more payments are still due in the same month. The "Sum (active, expected and not yet paid subscriptions)" total and the "Subscriptions to pay" widget on the home page stop counting the remaining occurrences.

## Affects

Users with a weekly subscription configured to skip 2 or more weeks between payments. Subscriptions that skip only 1 week are not affected.

## Cause

A calculation error in how Firefly III tracks the next due date for weekly subscriptions with a skip interval of 2 or more.

## Remedy

Update to Firefly III v6.7.0 or later, which fixes this calculation.
