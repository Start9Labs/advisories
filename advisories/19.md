# v1 backups are invalid on 0.4.0-beta.10 and newer

## Symptom

None until a restore is attempted. The old backup simply will not work.

## Affects

Anyone who updated past 0.4.0-beta.10 — in practice to 0.4.0 or 0.4.0.1 — and has not made a new backup since.

## Cause

The backup format changed. v1 backups made on 0.4.0-beta.9 or earlier cannot be restored on 0.4.0-beta.10 or newer.

## Remedy

Make a fresh v2 backup immediately after updating. It is a full backup, not differential on top of v1, so it takes a while and needs its own space — expect roughly double while both exist. Create the v2 backup first, then delete the v1 one; the create-backup page has a button for that. If the disk cannot hold both, use a different target, or accept the risk of deleting the v1 backup before creating the v2.

## Notes

Known since 2026-07-23.
