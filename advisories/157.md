# Bark Wallet: "Back Up Now" fails partway through with no explanation on slow or Tor-reached backup targets

## Symptom

Running Back Up Now, the backup starts, runs for a while, and then simply stops with no useful error — it is killed partway through, most often when backing up to an external target reached over Tor or a slow connection.

## Affects

Anyone backing up Bark Wallet to a target where the snapshot plus the rclone upload together take longer than 30 seconds — routine over Tor or a slow link. Not affected: backups to fast, local, or directly-attached targets that complete within 30 seconds.

## Cause

Back Up Now runs `backup-agent.sh --once` through `exec` with no explicit timeout, so it inherits the SDK's 30 second default and is killed as soon as that elapses, even though the script itself already bounds its own steps (sqlite's `.timeout`, rclone's `--contimeout`, `--timeout` and `--retries`).

## Remedy

This is fixed by removing the 30 second timeout so the backup runs to completion. The fix has merged and will be available once a release of Bark Wallet containing it reaches StartOS.

## Notes

The fix is bark-web 0.9.0:2, currently on the alpha registry only — not yet available on the main Start9 Registry.
