# Services must be updated for 0.4.0 compatibility after migrating

## Symptom

A service misbehaves or will not start after the migration.

## Affects

Every installed service after updating from 0.3.5.1 to 0.4.0.

## Cause

Services packaged for 0.3.5.1 need a 0.4.0-compatible build.

## Remedy

Update all services. Bitcoin Core will offer its latest 31.x by default; staying on 28.x, 29.x, or 30.x is supported, but the latest revision of that major version is still required. Pick it from the version dropdown at the top of the sidebar on Bitcoin Core's marketplace page.

## Notes

Known since 2026-07-23.
