# Forgejo Runner shows healthy while crash-looping and processing no jobs — update to 13.1.0:0

## Symptom

The Forgejo Runner service shows a green "Running" health status almost all the time, but it is actually crash-looping every couple of minutes and picking up no CI/CD jobs. Health checks appear to succeed (`success`) far more often than they should during a crash loop — one measurement showed `success` on 105 of 120 samples over ten minutes while the service restarted roughly every 2 minutes.

## Affects

Anyone running Forgejo Runner on a StartOS install where the runner's credentials (token) have become invalid or unset — for example after a bad reconfiguration, a revoked token, or any other cause of a crash loop. It is not limited to one trigger; the bug is in how health was judged, not in what caused the crash.

## Cause

The health check judged the runner ready by checking whether stored credentials (`runnerUuid`/`runnerToken`) were set, a value read once at startup — it never observed whether the daemon process was actually alive. Because the daemon's own health check overwrote the SDK's crash status on every poll, a crashing runner was repainted green roughly every 30 seconds.

## Remedy

Update Forgejo Runner to version 13.1.0:0. The health check now requires both a live `forgejo-runner` process and a working connection to the container engine, so a crash loop is correctly shown as failing (e.g. "The runner is not running") instead of green. No further action is needed after updating — if the runner was actually misconfigured (bad token), reconfigure it as usual; the health status will now accurately reflect whether that fixed it.

## Notes

The companion package `gitea-runner-startos` has the same underlying defect, tracked separately.
