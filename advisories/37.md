# Actual Budget: large budget files lag and flicker when editing transactions quickly — update to 26.8.1 or later

## Symptom

With a large budget file (tens of thousands of transactions), editing transactions quickly — changing categories, entering new transactions, duplicating, deleting, or toggling Cleared — causes edits to lag several seconds or "flicker" back and forth as if reverted. Payee autocomplete and new transaction entry can feel unresponsive, with edits appearing to not register and then landing seconds later.

## Affects

Self-hosted Actual Budget with large budget files (tens of thousands of transactions built up over years, e.g. migrated from YNAB). Not related to the sync server's performance — the same slowdown appears with no sync server configured. Small or typical-sized budget files are not affected.

## Cause

Certain actions (duplicate, delete, toggling Cleared, and — before a further fix — general edits) trigger a full refetch of the entire loaded transaction list rather than an incremental update, and the resulting payload is decoded and re-rendered on the main thread, blocking the UI.

## Remedy

Update to Actual Budget 26.8.1 or later, which shipped further speed improvements to this path. Multiple users confirmed edits became responsive again on the same large budget files after updating.

## Notes

- Reports after 26.8.1 (v26.9.0) indicate some slowness may remain on certain clients — Chrome/Firefox and mobile PWA are reported slower than the desktop app or Edge — so this may not be fully resolved for every platform; unconfirmed whether this is the same underlying cause or a separate one.
- The desktop app has consistently been reported as fast throughout.
