# Firefly III: viewing an account with an attachment fails with a 500 error — fix coming in 6.7.3

## Symptom

Opening the details ("show") page for an account fails with a 500 Internal Server Error. The underlying error is `FireflyIII\View\Components\Lists\Attachments::__construct(): Argument #1 ($attachments) must be of type Illuminate\Support\Collection|array, string given ... (View: /var/www/html/resources/views/accounts/show.blade.php)`.

## Affects

Any account that has at least one attachment uploaded to it. Accounts with no attachments are unaffected.

## Cause

A type mismatch when the account view renders its list of attachments.

## Remedy

This is fixed for the next release, 6.7.3. Until then, removing the attachment from the affected account restores access to its details page; re-adding an attachment reintroduces the error.

## Do not

- Do not treat this as data loss — the account and its attachment remain in the database; only the details page fails to render while an attachment is present.

## Notes

Confirmed by a Firefly III maintainer (JC5) and fixed, targeted for 6.7.3. StartOS currently ships 6.7.1 and 6.6.6, both of which predate this fix.
