# ERPNext: "% Amount Billed" exceeds 100% on fully rejected Purchase Receipts — fixed in 15.121.3

## Symptom

On a Purchase Receipt where the received items are fully rejected, the "% Amount Billed" field shows a value greater than 100%, when it should reflect that little or nothing is billable.

## Affects

Purchase Receipts with fully rejected quantities, on versions before 15.121.3.

## Remedy

Update to ERPNext 15.121.3 or later, which corrects the "% Amount Billed" calculation for fully rejected Purchase Receipts.
