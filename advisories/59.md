# Jellyfin 12.0: shows and seasons get stuck marked "watched" with wrong unwatched counts

## Symptom

After upgrading to Jellyfin 12.0, TV shows and seasons that are unwatched or only partially watched show up as fully watched — a black/filled checkmark on the poster, or an empty "0 unwatched" indicator — while the individual episodes underneath remain correctly marked unwatched (Next Up still points at the right next episode). Clicking the watched/unwatched toggle appears to work (the icon turns white) but reverts as soon as the page is refreshed or the show is reopened. The incorrect status is visible across all clients (web, Android, Roku, Windows) and across every user account on the server, including newly created ones — so it is not a per-user watch state or a UI/CSS issue. Unwatched episode counts shown for a season or series frequently do not match the sum of the actual unwatched episodes in it.

## Affects

Anyone who upgraded a Jellyfin library with existing watch history to 12.0. Still present on 12.1. Does not depend on folder structure, naming convention, or library settings — reported with several different layouts including both "flat" episode folders and "Season xx" subfolders.

## Cause

Unknown — under investigation upstream. Not a client display bug: the incorrect watched/unwatched state is stored server-side and is consistent across clients and user accounts.

## Remedy

There is no fix yet. For an affected show, triggering a metadata refresh with "Replace all metadata" sometimes restores the correct watched/unwatched state — but multiple users report it does not work for every show, and some remain stuck regardless. Rolling back to 10.11.11 and restoring a pre-12.0 backup has also been reported to resolve it, but that means giving up any 12.0-only changes made since upgrading.

## Do not

- Don't tell them to just manually toggle watched/unwatched — this visibly "works" in the UI but reverts on the next page load or refresh.

## Notes

Still open and unresolved as of 12.1; no maintainer-confirmed cause or fix version yet. Distinct from the "large playlist crashes on login" and "nested library folders" 12.0 issues already tracked — this is specifically about watched-status and episode-count accuracy, not a crash or misfiled content.
