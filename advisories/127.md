# StartOS upgrade fails with "defrag failed ... No space left on device" during ext4→btrfs conversion — re-run the upgrade

## Symptom

During a USB upgrade from StartOS 0.3.5.x that converts the `package-data` drive from ext4 to btrfs, the upgrade stops with a flood of errors like:

```
ERROR: defrag failed on /media/startos/data/package-data.convert-tmp/volumes/… : No space left on device
```

This looks like the data drive is full and invites deleting data to make room. It is not actually full: the ext4→btrfs conversion has already completed successfully by the time this error appears.

## Affects

Servers upgrading from 0.3.5.x to 0.4.0.x via USB upgrade, where the data drive is mostly full and has a lot of fragmented data (for example, years of accumulated Bitcoin block files). Drives with more free space or less fragmentation are less likely to hit it. Confirmed on StartOS 0.4.0.1 and reproduced against the current `master` (0.4.0.2 development).

## Cause

`btrfs-convert` leaves the original ext4 image in an `ext2_saved` subvolume that shares data extents with the converted files. StartOS deletes that subvolume, but the deletion only queues it for background cleanup — the extents stay allocated until that cleanup finishes. The upgrade then runs a full defragment of the converted filesystem, which needs fresh space for every rewritten file; with the old extents still pinned, that space isn't available yet, and the optional defrag step fails ENOSPC and aborts the upgrade even though the conversion itself is already valid.

## Remedy

Do not delete data. Simply re-run the same USB upgrade: it detects the drive is already converted to btrfs, skips the conversion step, and completes normally.

## Do not

- Chroot in and delete the blockchain or other data to "free space" — the drive was not actually low on usable space; the error is from the optional defrag step, not from lacking room for the conversion.

## Notes

A fix has merged (start9labs/start-technologies#4076): it waits for the deleted `ext2_saved` subvolume's cleanup to finish (`btrfs subvolume sync`) before defragmenting, only defragments when free space covers a second copy of the used data plus a 5%-or-1-GiB reserve, skips defrag entirely when that headroom is absent, and logs a warning instead of failing the upgrade if defrag still fails despite the headroom. It is unreleased as of this report; no version is confirmed to contain it yet. The fix will be available once a release containing it reaches StartOS.
