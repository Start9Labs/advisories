# Core Lightning 26.06.7: mutual close silently force-closes instead — "We agreed on a closing fee" followed by "Tracking our own unilateral close"

## Symptom

Channel status shows the close was negotiated successfully, then immediately falls back to a unilateral close:

```
"CLOSINGD_SIGEXCHANGE:We agreed on a closing fee of 195 satoshi for tx:777777",
"ONCHAIN:Tracking our own unilateral close",
```

No error is shown to the user; the channel simply closes on-chain via the commitment transaction instead of the cheaper, private mutual close.

## Affects

Core Lightning 26.06.7, on the node that opened the channel (opener only), when fee estimates are pinned at the fee floor so both sides' negotiation range collapses to a single value. Rounding of the two output amounts to whole satoshis can push the actual transaction fee 1 satoshi above that value, which the release's new fee-ceiling check then rejects. Roughly half of closes negotiated with no fee headroom hit this, since the rounding remainder is essentially a coin flip. Channels closed with normal fee headroom, or as the non-opening peer, are not affected.

## Cause

A new check in lightningd (26.06.7) compares the mutual close transaction's actual fee against the negotiated ceiling, but rounding during fee negotiation can put the real fee 1 satoshi over that ceiling. When the check rejects the transaction, lightningd does not tell closingd to renegotiate or abort — it silently falls through and broadcasts the previous, still-current commitment transaction instead, producing an unwanted force close.

## Remedy

Check the node log for the closed channel for the string `above our max` to confirm this is what happened.

When initiating a mutual close, specify a fee range whose top is clearly above the peer's expected fee (e.g. 253perkw to 5000perkw) rather than leaving it at the tight default, so the negotiated fee has headroom and the 1-satoshi rounding cannot push it over the ceiling.

## Do not

- Do not treat this as a wallet or fund-loss issue — the force close still resolves to the user's own funds, it is just an unnecessary, less private on-chain close with a delay before funds return.

## Notes

Reported as present in 26.06.7 and not present on master at time of report; no fixed version has shipped yet. Only the channel opener is affected, and only when the negotiated fee has no headroom above the floor.
