# Faces not detected in photos rotated through Immich's editor

## Symptom

After rotating a photo inside Immich (via the edit/rotate tool) to correct its orientation, face detection does not find faces in it — even after using "Refresh faces" on the photo. This mainly affects older photos with no EXIF rotation metadata that were uploaded already rotated the wrong way, then straightened using Immich's own rotate feature. Downloading the rotated image and re-uploading it as a new file does detect faces correctly, which points to the in-app rotation path specifically.

## Affects

Users who rotate a photo using Immich's built-in edit/rotate tool, particularly older images that lack EXIF orientation data. Users on Immich versions before v3.2.0. Photos that already had correct EXIF rotation data, or were re-uploaded after rotating externally, are not affected.

## Cause

Face detection and related jobs (OCR was also reported affected) ran against the original, unrotated image data rather than the edited orientation, so faces in the rotated version were never scanned.

## Remedy

Update to Immich v3.2.0 or newer, now available on StartOS, which includes the fix. After updating, re-run face detection on your library (Refresh faces) so previously rotated photos are rescanned — the fix does not retroactively re-scan old rotations on its own.

## Notes

After updating to v3.2.0, at least one user reports (unconfirmed) that face bounding boxes on previously-rotated photos appear offset or squished, and that featured/person photos may still display with incorrect rotation, even though detection itself now works. This may be a separate, related issue — if a customer reports misaligned face boxes after upgrading, note it but do not promise a fix.
