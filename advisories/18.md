# StartTunnel update spinner never dismisses

## Symptom

The "Installing StartTunnel" spinner runs forever.

## Affects

Updating StartTunnel from below 1.2 to 1.2 or newer.

## Cause

A UI bug in the 1.2.0 update flow. The update itself completes normally.

## Remedy

Wait one minute, then hard-refresh the page.

## Notes

Known since 2026-07-23.
