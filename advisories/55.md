# ERPNext: Dunning marked Resolved before its interest and fee are paid — fixed in 15.121.3

## Symptom

A Dunning created against an overdue Sales Invoice (with a Dunning Type that includes interest and a fee) is marked **Resolved** as soon as the invoice amount alone is paid, even though the dunning interest and fee remain unpaid. The **Create → Payment** option disappears from the dunning. Creating a new dunning from the same invoice shows an outstanding of 0, with Total Interest and Grand Total both 0.00, so the remaining interest and fee are effectively lost. Using Create → Payment on the original dunning instead fails with:

> Sales Invoice ACC-SINV-XXXXX has already been fully paid

## Affects

Users on ERPNext v15 or v16 raising a Dunning that includes interest and/or a fee, where the customer pays only the invoice amount and leaves the dunning interest/fee outstanding. Not seen when the invoice and dunning are settled together, or when the dunning has no interest/fee. Confirmed on ERPNext v15.77.0 (Frappe Framework v15.78.1).

## Cause

The dunning's resolution status and payment logic were tied only to the invoice's outstanding amount, so paying off the invoice alone caused the dunning to be treated as fully settled and closed off the ability to collect the remaining interest and fee.

## Remedy

Update to ERPNext 15.121.3 or later. After the update, a dunning stays Unresolved until both the invoice and its interest/fee are paid; Create → Payment on the dunning generates a Payment Entry for only the outstanding dunning amount (without re-referencing the already-paid invoice), and cancelling that payment reopens the dunning as claimable again.

## Notes

Dunnings created or resolved incorrectly before updating may need to be reviewed manually, since the fix changes how outstanding dunning amounts are calculated going forward.
